Install or update

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and download the APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps from this source.
  3. Choose Install or Update in the Android installer.

Already using Jelbi Editor? Install over the existing app to keep your history and draft. Do not uninstall it first.

What’s new in 1.3

  • A wider Before/After camera view, including 0.5× when your phone exposes a compatible lens. The available factor may vary; gallery import remains available.
  • A station-name guide and a crop preview before accepting the sign photo.
  • The Jelbi logo without the white rectangle.

Existing history images stay unchanged. Older draft previews need to be generated again; photos and quantities are kept.

Keep Play Protect enabled. If Android shows a security warning, stop and send its exact text or a screenshot, your phone model, Android version, country and the APK filename to the person who shared this page. A warning needs to be identified before it can be assessed.
