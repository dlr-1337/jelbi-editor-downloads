Personal phone: install or update

  1. Open this page on your own Android phone and download the APK from the button above.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks for permission to install from this source, allow only the browser or file manager you are using.
  3. If Play Protect blocks the app, stop and follow the security guidance below. Keep Play Protect enabled.
  4. Choose Install or Update in the Android installer.
  5. Wait for the installation to finish, then open Jelbi Editor and create and save a report.

Already using Jelbi Editor? Install over the existing app to keep your history and draft. Do not uninstall it first.

Company-managed phone

Ask your IT administrator to approve Jelbi Editor and distribute this APK through the company’s authorized app channel.

If the phone says “Action not allowed” or “Contact your IT admin”, installation is restricted by device management. This download link and QR code do not grant company approval.

Send IT this page, the APK and its checksum. IT should confirm whether an internal APK distribution channel outside Google Play is available under company policy.

Do not remove device management, switch profiles to bypass the restriction or turn off security protections.

What’s new in 1.3

  • A wider Before/After camera view, including 0.5× when your phone exposes a compatible lens. The available factor may vary; gallery import remains available.
  • A station-name guide and a crop preview before accepting the sign photo.
  • The Jelbi logo without the white rectangle.

Existing history images stay unchanged. Older draft previews need to be generated again; photos and quantities are kept.

Installation warnings

A browser download warning, permission to install from a source and a Play Protect block are different messages.

If Play Protect says it has not seen apps from this developer before, the warning corresponds to its Uncommon category. It needs to be assessed; it is not proof that an installation has been approved.

Stop and send the exact warning or a screenshot, phone model, Android/One UI version, country and APK filename to the person who shared this page. Mention whether this is a personal or company-managed phone. Do not select “Install anyway” as a workaround.

Keep Play Protect enabled. A successful download does not mean the phone permits installation. Company approval and Google’s app classification are separate checks. Neither this page nor a new download can override them.
